  KEYWORDS                             Abstract A boundary layer analysis has been presented to study the effects of thermal radiation,
  Thermal radiation;                   viscous dissipation, and Hall current effects on the hydromagnetic convection ﬂow of an electrically
  Viscous dissipation;                 conducting, viscous, incompressible ﬂuid past over a stretching vertical ﬂat plate. The governing
  Hall current effect;                 partial differential equations are non-dimensionalized and transformed into a system of nonlinear
  MHD;                                 ordinary differential similarity equations, in a single independent variable. The resulting coupled
  Stretched ﬂat plate                  nonlinear equations are solved under appropriate transformed boundary conditions using the
                                       Runge–Kutta fourth-order method along with the shooting method. The effects of the radiation
                                       parameter, magnetic parameter, Hall parameter, viscous dissipation parameter and the buoyancy
                                       parameter on the velocity proﬁles, the cross ﬂow velocity proﬁles and the temperature proﬁles
                                       are presented graphically and discussed.
