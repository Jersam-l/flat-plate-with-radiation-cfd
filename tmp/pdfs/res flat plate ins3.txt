Keywords:                                                    This study investigates an unsteady convective flow of a ternary nanofluid (MgO–TiO2–CoFe2O4/H2O) over an
Ternary nanofluid                                            infinite vertical plate through Darcy-Brinkman porous medium under the influences of a uniform transverse
Darcy-Brinkman flow                                          magnetic field, thermal radiation, Soret effect with different heat source/sinks. The novel contribution of this
Soret effect
                                                             study lies in examining the effects of suspending ternary nanoparticles on heat and mass transfer in fluid flow.
Thermal radiation
                                                             The governing coupled non-linear partial differential equations of the flow model are transformed into ordinary
Permeable medium
Heat source/sink                                             differential equations and solved using perturbation technique. Results reveal that ternary nanoparticles enhance
                                                             the skin friction coefficient from 0.38% to 0.54%, while the Nusselt number decreases from 0.18% to 0.73%, and
                                                             the Sherwood number increases significantly from 0.53% to 1.94%. The Soret effect significantly enhances
                                                             species diffusion, with higher Schmidt numbers reducing molecular diffusion rates. RSM-based predictive models
                                                             demonstrate excellent accuracy with R2 values of 97.49% for skin friction, 99.94% for heat transfer, and 96.57%
                                                             for mass transfer, validated through ANOVA.
